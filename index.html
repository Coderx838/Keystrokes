HOW TO PLAY

KEYBOARD

Home-row keys play white notes: A S D F G H J K L ; Black notes live just above them: W E T Y U O P

MOUSE & TOUCH

Just click any key on screen. Works on your phone too — tap the keys with a finger.

CONTROLS

SPACE = hold to sustain · Z / X = shift octave down / up · 1–8 = drum kit

TIP

Everything sits inside the same key, so mash it however you like — it still sounds good.
